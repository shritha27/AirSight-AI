About AirSight AI

AirSight AI is an open atmospheric intelligence platform engineered to bridge tropospheric chemistry models, satellite reanalysis, and ground sensor networks for environmental researchers and municipal decision-makers.

satellite_alt Primary Telemetry Providers
  • • Copernicus CAMS: Tropospheric composition reanalysis and aerosol optical depth assimilation.
  • • NOAA GML: Global monitoring laboratory baseline greenhouse gas and ozone profiles.
  • • EPA AirNow: Continuous 1-hour and 24-hour micro-particulate telemetry network.
  • • Sentinel-5P TROPOMI: High-resolution spatial column density for NO2 and tropospheric ozone.
verified_user Regulatory Conformance
  • • EPA 40 CFR Part 58: National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS) calculation protocol.
  • • ISO 14044: Environmental management and lifecycle air quality assessment guidelines.
  • • Zero Drift Calibration: Baseline sensors recalibrated with automated temperature and humidity regression.
