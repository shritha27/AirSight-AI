## Brand & Style
The design system operates at the intersection of rigorous atmospheric instrumentation and cinematic environmental editorial. It communicates meteorological transparency, forensic precision, and planetary scale without descending into sterile clinical data displays or synthetic greenwashing.

The visual style merges modern editorial minimalism with subtle atmospheric glassmorphic layering. Interfaces evoke natural light passing through changing atmospheric density: clean chalky off-whites, cool slate structural anchors, pale particulate hazes, and micro-diffused ambient light.

Key principles:
- **Atmospheric Clarity**: Scientific depth through low-contrast structure, high-precision typography, and ambient space rather than heavy container borders.
- **Forensic Honesty**: Environmental data is rendered truthfully. Avoid generic saturated leaf greens; healthy states leverage cool tropospheric slate and muted sage, while warnings surface through dusty coral and warm particulate ambers.
- **Cinematic Precision**: Information density is balanced by wide margins, delicate hairline separators, and typographic scale shifts reminiscent of high-end cartography and scientific journals.

## Colors
The palette captures physical atmospheric states: particulate scattering, tropospheric depth, and thermal shifts. Saturated baseline greens are intentionally omitted in favor of muted sage and crisp slate tones to preserve neutral, objective scientific authority.

### Semantic Mapping
- **Canvas (`#F7F7F4`)**: Soft particulate off-white canvas representing daylight ambient baseline.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for focal data panels, raised cards, and active inspection layers.
- **Primary Deep Midnight (`#25384A`)**: Used for core structural chrome, navigation anchors, key data visualizations, and primary actions.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Sky (`#A9D1E8`)**: Used for atmospheric flow maps, wind field vector highlights, selection fills, and ambient gradients.
- **Tertiary Coral Alert (`#E58D78`)**: Reserved strictly for hazardous particulate thresholds (PM2.5 / PM10 spikes, severe AQI alerts, volatile anomalies).
- **Primary Text (`#29333D`)**: Slate black for headlines, metric values, and primary textual content.
- **Muted Blue-Grey (`#8496A5`)**: For metadata labels, axis markers, inactive controls, and secondary units.
- **Misty Lavender (`#DCDDEB`)**: Atmospheric layer tint for inversion layer visualizations, subtle card hover states, and tag backgrounds.
- **Warm Sand (`#E7D8C5`)**: Used for moderate threshold indicators, thermal heat layer overlays, and dust/sand dispersion traces.
- **Muted Sage (`#A9B9A5`)**: Used for optimal air quality baselines and healthy ecological indicators without chromatic over-saturation.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E3E7EA`)**: Hairline boundary lines for cards and structured tables.

## Typography
Typographic expression bridges technical scientific instrument readouts with publication-grade editorial poise.

- **Headlines & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides geometric precision, subtle structural tension, and technical authority. All metric indicators (AQI, µg/m³, ppm, hPa) must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time telemetry streaming.
- **Body & Labels (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Ensures supreme micro-scale legibility across dense sensor grids, observational feeds, and tabular reporting sheets.
- **Uppercase Metadata**: Small labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use tracked uppercase styles (`text-transform: uppercase`) paired with subtle letter-spacing for coordinates, station identifiers, and physical units.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive fluid grid system designed for panoramic spatial telemetry and high-density telemetry dashboards.

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1680px max bounds, 28px (`gutter-desktop`) gutters, 48px (`margin-desktop`) outer padding. Allows 3-column multi-station comparison splits or an 8:4 sensor-map to telemetry-feed arrangement.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: 768px to 1023px, 20px (`gutter`) gutters, 24px (`margin`) outer padding. Panels stack into modular 4-column pairing blocks.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: 320px to 767px, 12px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters, 16px (`margin-mobile`) margins. Single-column stacked stream with horizontally scrolling atmospheric metric strips.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical light passing through varying densities of the atmosphere, balancing crisp boundaries with subtle optical diffusion.

- **Base Canvas**: Flat `#F7F7F4`, anchoring the entire interface.
- **Card Tier 1 (Surface Rest)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with an ultra-fine structural border (`1px solid #E3E7EA`) and a feather-light ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(37, 56, 74, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 56, 74, 0.06)`.
- **Card Tier 2 (Atmospheric Float / Hover)**: Elevated data modules transition to `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(37, 56, 74, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(37, 56, 74, 0.03)`.
- **Glassmorphic Overlays (HUD / Map Overlays / Floaters)**: Semi-transparent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`) backed by a dynamic backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`), bounded by a subtle tinted inner hairline border (`1px solid rgba(227, 231, 234, 0.85)`).
- **Critical Alerts & Overlays**: Modals and hazardous warning flyouts maintain solid `#FFFFFF` grounding, reinforced by a sharp tinted edge (`#E58D78` hairline top accent) and a deep diffused ground projection: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(37, 56, 74, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
Shapes employ balanced, modern architectural corners (`roundedness: 2`). This provides a refined, contemporary stance that avoids both archaic clinical squared corners and overly playful pill-heavy consumer conventions.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Segmented Controls)**: 8px border-radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Surface Cards & Data Grids**: 16px border-radius (`1rem`).
- **Modal Containers & Atmospheric Map Overlays**: 24px border-radius (`1.5rem`).
- **Telemetry Chips & Status Badges**: Strictly capped at 6px (`0.375rem`) to maintain the appearance of scientific hardware tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Midnight (`#25384A`) fill, crisp white text, 8px radius. Hover state lightens to a subtle luminous navy (`#31475D`) with an ambient 4px shadow. Active state depresses 1px down.
- **Secondary / Atmospheric**: Translucent Sky Blue tint (`rgba(169, 209, 232, 0.22)`) background with Midnight text (`#25384A`) and a subtle boundary line (`#A9D1E8`).
- **Ghost / Utility**: Transparent background, `#8496A5` text, shifting to `#29333D` text with a `#F7F7F4` background fill on hover.

### Sensor Chips & Environmental Badges
- Compact rectangular modules (6px radius) displaying pollutant species (`PM2.5`, `O₃`, `NO₂`).
- Text in uppercase `label-sm` with tabular numeric readout.
- **Status Tints**:
  - *Clean / Baseline*: Pale Muted Sage tint background (`rgba(169, 185, 165, 0.2)`) with deep slate-sage text.
  - *Moderate / Hazy*: Warm Sand tint background (`rgba(231, 216, 197, 0.3)`) with warm umber-slate text.
  - *Hazardous / Surge*: Coral Alert tint background (`rgba(229, 141, 120, 0.18)`) with Coral text (`#C95A41`).

### Cards & Telemetry Panels
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, 16px radius, enclosed by 1px `#E3E7EA` hairline borders.
- Header zones partition metric category and live timestamp with subtle 1px bottom border dividers.
- Metric values dominate the visual hierarchy with `metric-xl` Space Grotesk styling.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E3E7EA` border, 8px radius.
- Placeholder text in `#8496A5`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#25384A` with a subtle focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(169, 209, 232, 0.35)`).

### Atmospheric Vector Sliders & Toggles
- Sliders feature an ultra-slim track (`4px`) colored in gradient `#A9D1E8` to `#25384A`.
- Draggable thumb is a crisp 18px pure white circle with a 1px border and a precise center dot of `#25384A`.
- Toggle switches use `#DCDDEB` when off and `#25384A` when engaged.

### Atmospheric HUD Overlay (Domain-Specific)
- Floating telemetry controller designed for interactive map surfaces.
- Constructed with frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, 16px blur) and 12px radius.
- Houses layer-toggles (wind shear vectors, particulate heatmaps, tropospheric humidity).
